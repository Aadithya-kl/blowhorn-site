## How it works

Major Blowhorn briefs the launch: one launch, four fronts, zero excuses.

1. **Pick a formation.** Muster, Battalion Map, One Thunder or Call to Arms. Each one changes who sends each post.
2. **Load the queue.** Add a sample post, or write your own. The board fills as you add.
3. **Sound the call.** The General calls the launch, and each post goes out in turn, starting tomorrow at 9:00.

This is a simulation. Nothing is posted, and nothing leaves your browser.
